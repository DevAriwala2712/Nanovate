About Nanovate Review

Nanovate Review — viewer for the training-free SEM topology verifier.

All patches are synthetic testbed samples; ground-truth labels come from exact topology of the wafer W vs CAD. Verdicts come from the full verifier (Modules 1, 3, 4) with the config frozen on Manhattan val (results/v2/calib/decisions.json). Nanovate analyses one patch at a time; it does not scan wafers or control any tool.
